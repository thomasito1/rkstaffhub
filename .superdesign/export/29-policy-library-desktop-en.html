Updated Tip Reporting Policy

Version 2.0 · last edited by Denise Lasarte on Aug 6, 2026

1. Tip Reporting Requirements

All employees at Rosalia's Kitchen who receive cash or credit card tips are required by federal law and restaurant policy to report 100% of their tip income. This ensure accurate tax withholding and compliance with IRS regulations.

Procedures for reporting:

  • All cash and credit card tips must be entered into the POS system or designated reporting tool at the time of clock-out for every shift.
  • Reported tip amounts are used for payroll tax withholding and will appear on your bi-weekly pay stub.
  • If an error is made during the reporting process, employees may only correct the reported amount through an active Manager or the General Manager.
  • Failure to report tips accurately, or falsifying tip reports, is considered a serious policy violation and is grounds for immediate disciplinary action, up to and including termination.

By signing this policy, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to comply with the tip reporting standards of Rosalia's Kitchen.
