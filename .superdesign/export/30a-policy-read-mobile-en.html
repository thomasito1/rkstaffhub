Updated Tip Reporting Policy

Version 2.0 · Aug 15, 2026 · 2 min

1. Reporting your tips

All cash and credit card tips must be reported accurately at the conclusion of every shift. Employees are required to use the digital reporting module within the Hub during the clock-out process. Failure to report tips daily may result in payroll discrepancies.

Reported totals are utilized for payroll tax withholding in accordance with IRS regulations. Rosalia’s Kitchen ensures that all withheld amounts are clearly documented on your bi-weekly pay stubs for your records.

2. Correcting a reported amount

In the event that a reporting error is made, corrections cannot be performed by the staff member once the shift has been officially closed. You must contact a manager immediately to request an adjustment.

All management adjustments require a written reason to be recorded in the system audit log. This maintains transparency and ensures our compliance with labor and tax auditing standards.

3. Accuracy

  • •

    Tip reports are cross-referenced daily against POS transaction records and punch records to ensure consistency across all departments.

  • •

    Repeated discrepancies between physical receipts and digital reports will be reviewed with a manager and may lead to a performance review.

  • •

    Falsifying a tip report is considered a serious violation of company policy and is grounds for immediate disciplinary action, up to termination.
